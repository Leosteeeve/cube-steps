# V1.3 Advanced CFOP validation

Advanced CFOP is a **preview**. Its physical-cube teaching acceptance is pending. The owner's earlier acceptance of LBL and Beginner CFOP does not cover the new formulas. No real mobile hardware or assistive-technology session has been measured in this release.

## Scope and independent admission

- Three separate methods remain selectable. Advanced has Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL and AUF, with an introduction plus five lessons.
- The cross table enumerates all **190,080** labeled white-edge coordinates. Complete graph checks, distance-decreasing transitions and an independent physical depth-three BFS agree; its maximum distance is eight face turns. Half turns count once; regrips are separate.
- The versioned 190,080-byte distance asset has SHA-256 `e9bfbe91bcaea8e85ce4dd56b350969adce7b058633be1f6175b01703c070f73`. Corruption, truncation, stale schema and unavailable/aborted loads fail explicitly.
- **41 F2L cases** are admitted against independent legal piece placements, with **240** oriented/source/low-rotation variants. Preparation covers all 1,536 legal target placement/orientation combinations across four slots. Every completed maneuver preserves the cross and already-completed pairs.
- **57 OLL cases** cover 215 unsolved yellow-orientation signatures plus one solved skip. Formula admission uses independently transcribed yellow-sticker directions and matched-parity permutation variations.
- **21 PLL cases** cover 71 unsolved relative signatures and all 288 legal last-layer permutations; four need only AUF. Formula admission starts from independently transcribed side-sticker patterns.
- **318 formulas** and **972 physical setup variations** are shared by solving, lessons, the library and practice. Formula-based cases are not identified by inverting that same formula. Offline generic search only creates six-face setup sequences for already-admitted states.
- All 119 named course examples are solved through the actual Advanced rules and checked against the recorded case and checkpoint, along with cross and AUF examples.
- References, numbering, original recognition prose and exact selection weights are documented in [the algorithm catalog](../algorithms.md). No tutorial images or videos are distributed.

The exact-cross proof concerns the four white edges. Finite-case admission and seeded full-solve tests are regression evidence, not a proof over every legal full-cube input.

## Fixed efficiency corpus

Seeds 1–1,000 use `seededScramble` in `tests/fixtures/lbl-cases.ts`. For each seed, Advanced solves the cross and runs its F2L from that resulting state. Beginner F2L is then run from the **identical state and holding frame**, retaining its own required rotations. Full-solve comparisons below start both methods from the original scramble.

Metrics use one for outer/wide turns (including half turns), two outer-block equivalents for slice moves, and a separate count for whole-cube rotations. Median and p95 use nearest-rank percentiles. Formula boundaries are retained; adjacent turns are not silently canceled across teaching actions.

| Measurement | Advanced mean / median / p95 | Beginner mean / median / p95 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F2L face-turn equivalents | 30.458 / 31 / 36 | 45.527 / 46 / 56 |
| F2L rotations | 0 / 0 / 0 | 5.494 / 5 / 7 |
| Full-solve face-turn equivalents | 61.677 / 62 / 70 | 109.733 / 111 / 129 |
| Full-solve rotations | 1.535 / 1 / 3 | 10.228 / 10 / 12 |

All three unchanged gates passed in the initial 147.68-second run: Advanced F2L mean turns at most 90% of Beginner (observed about 66.9%), median no higher, and mean rotations no higher. Every cross length equaled its verified minimum. The full-solve totals are reported separately and are not substituted for the F2L gates. The `f2l-v1` heuristic does not claim globally shortest F2L or measured human execution speed.

## Desktop performance

The benchmark command is `node scripts/benchmark-advanced.mjs`, using the built production worker and actual UI requests. On an **AMD Ryzen 9 8945HX**, Windows **10.0.26200**, Node **24.18.0**, Chromium **156.0.8078.4**, it recorded 20 fresh-context cold runs, 50 cached warm worker requests, and 20 immediate method-switch cancellations. Measurements use local HTTP, not a public-network download or real mobile hardware. The candidate build was based on `3ef3aa5` plus the Task 13 changes; raw samples are preserved with release evidence.

| Duration in milliseconds | Mean | Median | p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cold navigation to successful reply | 298.34 | 284.10 | 356.30 |
| Cold worker request | 121.04 | 113.30 | 176.00 |
| Warm worker request | 107.85 | 105.20 | 158.10 |
| Method switch to worker termination | 0.66 | 0.60 | 0.90 |

Cold timing includes loading the page, entering a fixed scramble, and clicking Explain. Warm timing runs from worker postMessage to its successful reply and reuses the browser HTTP cache; workers remain disposable. Cancellation measures immediate method switching. An initial benchmark harness used raw button text and missed an aria-hidden arrow; its locator was corrected before collecting this complete run.

Engineering targets are cold p95 at most 5 seconds and warm worker p95 at most 2 seconds on the named desktop. The 30-second watchdog is an error safeguard. Browser viewport emulation is layout evidence only.
